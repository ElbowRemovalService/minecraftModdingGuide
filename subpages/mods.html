Where to get mods

  • Modrinth
  • CurseForge
  • The author's own GitHub or site, linked from one of the above
Mod files are programs. A malicious mod can do anything your account can. Only download from trusted sources.

Warning signs

  • A download from a site that is not the mod's official page.
  • An .exe or installer where a .jar is expected.
  • Ad-heavy "mod download" sites and file-sharing links.
